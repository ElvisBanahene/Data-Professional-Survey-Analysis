Data Professional Survey Analysis | Power BI
Project Overview
How are professionals in the data industry doing? What are their salaries, favorite programming languages, and experiences breaking into the field? How satisfied are they with their work-life balance and compensation?

This project explores these questions through an interactive Power BI dashboard built using real survey data from professionals in the data industry.

The project involved cleaning and transforming raw survey data using Power Query and developing interactive visualizations to explore career experiences, salary trends, programming language preferences, and job satisfaction.

The goal was to transform raw survey data into an accessible visual report that helps users explore different aspects of working in the data industry.

 Project Objectives
The main objectives of this project were to:

Explore the geographical distribution of survey respondents.
Compare average salaries across different job titles.
Identify respondents' favorite programming languages.
Examine how difficult respondents found it to break into the data industry.
Explore reported work-life balance ratings.
Investigate salary satisfaction among survey participants.
Build an interactive dashboard that presents survey information clearly and effectively.

 Dashboard Features
The dashboard contains several visualizations designed to explore different aspects of the data profession.

1. Country of Respondents 
A treemap showing the geographical distribution of survey participants across countries, including Canada, India, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

2. Average Salary by Job Title 
A bar chart comparing average salaries across different job roles, including Data Scientists, Data Engineers, Data Architects, Data Analysts, and other categories.

This visualization helps users explore differences in average salary across roles represented in the survey.

3. Favorite Programming Language 
A chart exploring respondents' preferred programming languages, including Python, R, and other programming languages represented in the dataset.

4. Difficulty Breaking into Data 
A visualization examining how respondents rated the difficulty of entering the data industry.

This provides a way to explore participants' reported experiences when pursuing careers in data.

5. Work-Life Balance 
A gauge displaying the average work-life balance rating reported by survey participants.

6. Salary Satisfaction 
A gauge presenting the average salary satisfaction rating reported by respondents.

7. Survey Summary 
Summary cards displaying the number of survey responses represented in the dashboard and the average age of respondents.

Limitations
The dashboard reflects the responses included in the survey dataset. The results may not represent the entire data industry, and findings should be interpreted in the context of the survey's sample size, methodology, and respondent characteristics.

Salary comparisons may also be influenced by factors such as country, job responsibilities, and professional experience.
